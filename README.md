# Growth Audit — Scroll The Bible

**Live:** https://hribeiro-adapty.github.io/scroll-the-bible-flow-rebuild/

Five ranked ideas for the Scroll The Bible onboarding and paywall, plus a clickable prototype rebuilt in Scroll The Bible's own design. Only the sequence changes. The art direction stays yours.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
|---|------|-------------------|
| 01 | Name their goal on the price screen | CR +15–20% |
| 02 | Offer the monthly plan when they close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 03 | Say the price on the first paywall page | CR +5–10% |
| 04 | Put your 4.8 next to the price | CR +5–10% |
| 05 | Ask what they want before the tour | CR +5–15% |

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps, not measured lift for Scroll The Bible. Every price, rating, quote and feature line comes from Scroll The Bible's own App Store listing, thejcbsnbrand.com and in-app screens. Prices are the US App Store prices.
